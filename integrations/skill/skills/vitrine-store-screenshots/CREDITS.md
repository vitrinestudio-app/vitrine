# Credits

This skill was written for Vitrine Studio. Its rules were distilled – not copied – from public
store guidance and from these MIT-licensed skills, which are worth reading:

- [ParthJadhav/app-store-screenshots](https://github.com/ParthJadhav/app-store-screenshots) –
  narrative arc, layout variation, cross-screen rules, quality bar
- [adamlyttleapps/claude-skill-aso-appstore-screenshots](https://github.com/adamlyttleapps/claude-skill-aso-appstore-screenshots) –
  benefit discovery, screenshot rating
- [truongduy2611/app-screenshots-cli-skills](https://github.com/truongduy2611/app-screenshots-cli-skills)
  (appshots-best-practices) – type ratios, text expansion
- [rshankras/claude-code-apple-skills](https://github.com/rshankras/claude-code-apple-skills)
  (screenshot-planner) – storyboard, creative assets

Primary sources: Apple "App Store asset best practices", App Review Guidelines 2.3.3 / 2.3.10,
Google Play "Add preview assets to showcase your app".
