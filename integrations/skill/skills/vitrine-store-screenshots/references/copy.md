# Copy

## Rules

1. One idea per headline. No "and" joining two features – split into two pages.
2. Short, common words. No jargon unless the audience uses it.
3. 3–5 words per line, max two lines. Break lines on purpose, with similar line widths.
4. Outcome over mechanism ("Receipts sort themselves", not "AI expense categorization").
5. The headline enhances the visual, it doesn't describe it.
6. No CTA, no price, no superlatives, no questions, no exclamation marks.
7. One second at arm's length: if you can't parse it, rewrite it.

## Three angles – write all three, pick one

| Angle | Does | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Paint a moment | reader imagines doing it | Check the weather before you wake up. |
| State an outcome | life afterwards | Your money, finally clear. |
| Kill a pain | names a problem, removes it | Never lose a streak again. |

## Formulas by page job

Hero: `[Outcome], without [pain].` · `The [adjective] way to [verb] [noun].` ·
`Your [noun],\n[benefit].` · `Every [noun],\n[outcome].`

Differentiator: `Only [App] [does X].` · `Built for [specific person].` ·
`[Verb] it once.\n[Outcome] forever.`

Feature: `[Verb] [noun] in [time].` · `See [thing] at a glance.` · `[Noun], sorted.` ·
`From [before] to [after].` · `Right on your [surface].`

Proof (App Store only, only if true): `[Number] [people] use [App].` · `Loved by [group].` ·
`Private by design.`

Closer: feature wall (4–6 single words) · `Made for people who [care about X].` · a quiet
statement.

Eyebrow labels (optional, 1–3 words): category (`WIDGETS`, `OFFLINE`), position (`01 / 05`),
time (`EVERY MORNING`). They orient; they never carry the benefit.

## Weak → better

| Weak | Better |
|---|---|
| Track habits and stay motivated | Keep your streak alive |
| Organize recipes with tags and favorites | Dinner, decided |
| Seamlessly sync across all devices | Start on phone. Finish on Mac. |
| Powerful analytics dashboard | See what changed this week |
| Welcome to MyApp | (cut – say what the app does) |
| The best meditation app | Two minutes to calm |

Red flags: seamless, powerful, revolutionary, all-in-one, next-generation, "AI-powered" as
the whole message, any line that still works with a competitor's name.

## Localizing copy

- Rewrite for the market; don't translate word by word.
- Length vs. English: German headlines +60–80 % (body text +30–35 %); Finnish, Polish,
  Brazilian Portuguese similar; French +15–25 %; CJK usually shorter but needs a CJK font;
  Arabic/Hebrew right-to-left.
- Design text boxes for the longest language. In Vitrine prefer, in this order: a shorter
  rewrite → automatic hyphenation for that language → a per-language override of size or box.
  Don't silently shrink one language far below the others.
- German: decide du/Sie once per set (consumer apps usually du). Avoid long compounds in
  headlines; if unavoidable, allow hyphenation.
- Give translators the page's job and a maximum length (`strings_export {format, path, context: true}`, CSV or XLIFF).
- Review every language as rendered images, not as strings.
